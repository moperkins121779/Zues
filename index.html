Enclosed area—
Floors: each floor keeps its own separate drawing. Switch floors any time, or add a new one.

Draw: click and drag on empty grid to place a wall. It snaps to 0°/45°/90°. Pick Exterior or Interior first — they're colored differently.

Move: grab the middle of a wall to slide it, or an amber corner dot to reshape it. Get close to another corner and it locks together.

Add-ons: pick a button, then click a wall. Drag a door/window along its wall to move it sideways. Drag its amber dot along the dashed centerline to move it up or down. Set the door's hinge/swing before placing it — click "Flip hinge/swing" later via right-click if you change your mind.

Curved lines: draw a straight baseline, then drag its violet center dot to bow it into an arc. Click the "R" label to type an exact R10' (radius), D20' (diameter), A90 (angle in degrees), or a plain number for the arc height.

Doors: click one to swing it open or closed. Double-click it to resize.

Resize windows/porches: double-click one and type width x height (windows: width x height x sill), like 3' x 6'-8".

Wiring & plumbing: pick a color/type, then drag across the plan like drawing a wall.

Remove things: click most items to remove them; right-click anything for a delete menu.

Set exact length: double-click the box on any wall's length, type a length, hit Enter.
Try formats like 12'6, 12'-6", or just 150".
